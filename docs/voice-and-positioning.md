# Voice, Positioning, And MVP

## 10-Second Promise

Map the moment that pulled you back to smoking, then get one calm restart move for today.

## Category

A relapse recovery and restart-planning app for people quitting smoking.

## Differentiation

Most quit-smoking apps emphasize streaks, health countdowns, scare facts, or generic tracking. This product focuses on the specific moment where freedom collapsed into permission, then gives a restart path without shame.

## Emotional Contract

The user should never feel:
- stupid;
- weak;
- lectured;
- medically judged;
- punished for honesty;
- trapped in a forever dashboard.

The user should feel:
- seen;
- clear;
- less alone;
- capable of a small restart;
- free from the drama of "starting over from zero."

## MVP Loop

Learn -> self-check -> interpretation -> micro-action -> save return plan

## Streak And Gamification Principle

The product can include a daily streak, but the streak must never become the moral center of the app. It should represent continuity after a decision, not personal worth.

Recommended model:
- decision date: the day the user decided to stop again;
- clean streak: full calendar days since the decision with no smoking;
- milestone badges: 1 day, 3 days, 7 days, 14 days, 30 days, 90 days;
- level language: grounded and adult, not childish;
- relapse action: record honestly, restart the decision date, keep the learning report.

The app should say:
- "Restart today";
- "Protect the next 24 hours";
- "The streak is information, not identity."

The app should not say:
- "You lost everything";
- "You failed";
- "Back to zero" as shame language.

## MVP Sections

- Start: thesis, restart stance, immediate action.
- Map: causal loop of relapse and return.
- Check-in: short assessment.
- Report: ranked loop, interpretation, recommended action.
- Streak: decision date, days clean, level, next badge, relapse-safe restart.
- Library: concise micro-actions and source notes.
