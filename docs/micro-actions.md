# Micro-Actions Library

## Immediate Reset

Use when the user smoked recently and feels the day is lost.

Action:
- Throw away or move the next easiest cigarette source.
- Drink water.
- Write the exact sentence that gave permission.
- Decide the next smoke-free hour only.

Source strength: evidence-backed for removing triggers and short-term coping; product assumption for phrasing.

## False Benefit Reframe

Use when smoking felt like relief, reward, or pause.

Action:
- Name the promised benefit.
- Ask: "What did it actually solve after 10 minutes?"
- Choose a replacement pause that is not framed as punishment.

Source strength: expert-inspired and product assumption.

## High-Risk Moment Script

Use before social pressure, alcohol, stress, or routine triggers.

Action:
- Write one line: "I do not smoke anymore; I am taking a minute."
- Decide where your hands go.
- Decide what you do for the first 90 seconds of craving.

Source strength: evidence-backed for trigger planning; product assumption for script wording.

## No Perfect Restart

Use when the user waits for Monday, a clean date, or a better mood.

Action:
- Pick a 2-hour clean window starting now.
- Make one environment change.
- Read one personal reason for freedom.

Source strength: product assumption based on relapse loop design.

## Support Escalation

Use when cravings feel intense, repeated, or unsafe to handle alone.

Action:
- Contact a quitline, clinician, pharmacist, counselor, or trusted person.
- Consider evidence-backed cessation supports such as nicotine replacement or prescribed medication with professional guidance.

Source strength: evidence-backed.
